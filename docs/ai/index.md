# AI & AI tools

LLM-based AI has changed a lot in IT and cybersecurity. Tools and applications get built faster than ever, even by people who aren't developers, and they keep getting more complex. The downside is that security risks grow with it. AI makes mistakes of its own, and too often the output is used without anyone supervising or checking it.

AI isn't only a development helper either. It also talks to people and takes over workloads, chatbots being the most common example. These come with their own set of risks.

So it's worth understanding how AI works and how to use it in a safe and productive way. That's what this section is about.
