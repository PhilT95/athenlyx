# Cloud hosting

Cloud hosters like Azure, AWS, GCP or smaller ones like Hetzner OVHCloud or many others are the backbone of modern infrastructure. They provide an easy way to set up scalable, dynamic and global systems that fit your every need. And they come with many advantages over hosting servers yourself:

- Add or remove capacity in minutes instead of buying hardware for peak load.
- Pay for what you use instead of investing in servers and data center space.
- The provider takes care of hardware, power, cooling and often patching.
- Multiple zones and regions make redundancy and disaster recovery straightforward.
- Spin up new environments with Infrastructure as Code and deploy close to your users worldwide.

## EU Hosting

Nowadays with GDPR and other Data privacy and Compliance-related topics to watch out for, the need for EU-centered Hosting alternatives next to the big US Hosters like Azure, AWS or GCP are more important than ever.

One of these hosters is Hetzner, a well-known german Hosting Company with data centers even outside the European Union. Their feature set is rather limited compared to bigger ones like StackIT, but their pricing is very competitive and is a real alternative for smaller to mid-sized companies.
